Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Nudgr ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the Nudgr iOS app handles your information.

Information We Collect

Nudgr does not require you to create an account. We do not collect personal information such as your name or email address.

The app uses your device location in the background to track journey progress and deliver milestone notifications. Location data is processed on-device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Advertising

Nudgr displays ads served by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdMob and AppLovin. These partners may collect or receive certain device identifiers (such as the Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA) and usage data to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance.

On iOS, we request your permission via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before any tracking identifier is shared with these partners. You can change your tracking preference at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

For more information about how our advertising partners handle your data:

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • AppLovin: legal.applovin.com/privacy

Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob and AppLovin for advertising. Please refer to their privacy policies linked above for details on the data they may collect.

Data Storage

Reminder data is stored locally on your device using iOS standard storage. We do not have access to your reminders.

Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob for advertising. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for details on data they may collect.

Children's Privacy

Nudgr is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
